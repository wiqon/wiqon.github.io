        Methodology

        How we decide what survives

        It's not luck, it's a process. Every idea goes through the same filters before it becomes a tool or a data point.

        1. 1

          Hypothesis

          We take a concrete market idea.

        2. 2

          Exact rules

          Entries, exits and size, with no ambiguity.

        3. 3

          Backtest with costs

          Years of data, fees, slippage and swaps.

        4. 4

          Out of sample

          We validate it on data we didn't use to build it: other periods and a real broker with its costs.

        5. 5

          Live, if it survives

          Only then does it become a product or service.
