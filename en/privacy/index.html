Privacy policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

1. Who we are

WIQON (wiqonlab.com) is a market research and software lab based in Paraguay. Data controller: Avelino González Melgarejo, on behalf of WIQON. Contact for any privacy matter: contacto@wiqonlab.com.

2. What data we keep

  • If you create an account: your email, the date you accepted this policy, the page language and whether you agreed to receive news. The authentication service also records the creation date and last sign-in.
  • We don't ask for your name, ID, phone, bank details or passwords.
  • If you write to us by email, WhatsApp or the contact form, we keep that conversation to answer you.

3. What we use it for

  • Giving you access to the sections for registered users (legal basis: performing the service you requested).
  • Sending you WIQON news, only if you ticked that box (legal basis: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time).
  • We don't sell or rent your data, and we don't use it for third-party advertising.

4. Who processes the data

  • Supabase hosts the accounts and sends the sign-in links, as a data processor.
  • GitHub Pages and Cloudflare serve the site and the domain.
  • These providers may process data outside Paraguay or Brazil, with their own security measures.

5. Third-party services on the site

  • Interactive charts and market tables are provided by TradingView and load when you reach those sections.
  • YouTube videos load only if you press play (privacy-enhanced mode).
  • Live prices come from Binance's public servers; fonts from Google Fonts.
  • When that content loads, your browser connects to those services, which have their own policies.

6. Storage in your browser

We don't use cookies. To know how many people visit the site we use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and keeps no data that identifies you (it counts visits, pages and country in aggregate). We store in your browser (local storage) only your session, the light or dark theme and whether you're signed in, so the page works.

7. How long

For as long as your account exists. If you ask us to delete it, we erase your data within 15 days, except what the law requires us to keep.

8. Your rights

You can ask at any time for access to your data, correction, account deletion, portability, or to withdraw your consent to receive news. If you are in Brazil, these rights are provided by the General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law 13,709/2018). Write to contacto@wiqonlab.com from your account's email and we reply within 15 days.

9. Minors

Accounts are for people over 18. If we detect an account belonging to a minor, we delete it.

10. Changes

If we change this policy, we update the date above and, if the change is significant, we notify registered users.
