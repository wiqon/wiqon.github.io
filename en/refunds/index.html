2. Returns and refunds

Requests must be made within 7 calendar days of delivery.

Digital book. Full refund if the file doesn't open or is incomplete and we can't send you a correct one, or if you were charged twice. Purchases made on Hotmart are also covered by Hotmart's guarantee, requested from your Hotmart account within the period shown there; Amazon purchases follow Amazon's return policy.

WIQON Shield license. Since the demo is the same program, free and with no time limit, there are no change-of-mind refunds once the key has been delivered. You do get a full refund if the license doesn't work on the account you gave us and we don't fix it within 7 days of your claim, if you were charged twice, or if you paid and haven't received the key yet (you can cancel before delivery). When a license is refunded, the key is void and must not be used.

Assisted installation. Full refund if it wasn't carried out, or if for reasons on our side the EA isn't left working. You can reschedule with notice.

Strategy audit. Full refund if you cancel before we start. If you cancel once work has begun, we refund the part not done according to the accepted proposal.

3. How to request a refund

Write to contacto@wiqonlab.com or WhatsApp +595 987 685 651 with your name, the product, the date and the payment receipt. We reply within 48 business hours and, where applicable, refund through the same payment method within 10 business days. See also the terms and conditions.
