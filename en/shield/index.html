What it does

  • Decides once a day, on the closed daily candle
  • Buys if the close is above the 100-period average and exits if it falls below
  • Long only, no leverage of its own
  • Shows its state in a panel on the chart
  • Doesn't send your data to WIQON: it only uses your broker's prices

Honest limitations

  • It doesn't guarantee profits. Its goal is to limit large drawdowns.
  • It earns less than buy and hold in markets that only go up.
  • On CFDs, swaps reduce the edge: in the test they took about a third of the gain.
  • It doesn't react to intraday drops.
  • It loses on most trades (21% winners): it wins with a few big rallies.

Frequently asked questions

What do I need to use it?

MetaTrader 5 (free) and an account with a broker that offers BTCUSD. To trade every day it's best to have a PC that stays on or a VPS. Try it on demo first with your broker's costs (swaps).

Does it guarantee profits?

No. No software can guarantee them. Its goal is to limit large drawdowns; in markets that only go up it earns less than buy and hold. We publish the good and the bad results in the report.
