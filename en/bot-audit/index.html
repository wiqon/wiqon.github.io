What we review

  • Real costs

    Fees, spread, funding and swaps: what the bot's dashboard doesn't always subtract.

  • What it competes against

    Your result against buy and hold, and against leaving the dollars in a yield-bearing stablecoin.

  • The worst drawdown

    How much it lost at its worst, how long it took to recover and what a crash like 2022 would have done.

    Coins picked with hindsight, favorable periods or trades that wouldn't actually have filled.

What we've already found in our lab

  • Spot grid bots (64 settings, 2018-2026)None beat buy and hold. The dashboard showed +237% “grid profit” while the real account ended at −56%.
  • DCA bots with safety orders (36 settings)Median +19% a year vs +23% buy and hold, with −80% drawdowns. In bear markets the bot gets stuck for up to 2.8 years, and the dashboard, which only counts closed deals, never shows the loss.
  • Arbitrage across 6 exchanges69 opportunities in 24 hours; almost none survived fees and transfer time.
  • Collecting the funding rate7–10% a year historically, almost all from 2021; 2–3% in 2025 and 2026.
  • Altcoin rotation33.7% a year with today's coins, 1.0% with 2021's: survivorship bias inflates everything.
