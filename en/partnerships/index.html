Brand partnerships

Comparative analysis, step-by-step guide, video series, an audit of a strategy or bot, or technical advice to tokenize a store's gift vouchers and digital warranties (see the lab, in Spanish).

Audience

  • People in Paraguay, Brazil and Argentina who trade or want to start trading crypto, with a focus on risk management.
  • Channels: wiqonlab.com, YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, X and Telegram, in Spanish (@wiqonlab) and Portuguese (@wiqonbr).
  • We share up-to-date metrics for each channel when you contact us.

Our rules

  • Content is labeled as a paid partnership.
  • We publish the real results. The brand may review the data before publication to fix errors, but not change the outcome.
  • We do not promote leveraged futures, guaranteed returns or products without an identifiable company behind them.
  • We check every company (registration, licenses and regulator warnings) before accepting.
  • We work with a written contract. We never make deposits to "activate" a partnership.

How we start

  1. Tell us the product, format and timing.
  2. We reply within 2 business days with a proposal and metrics.
  3. We sign the contract and start the test. The usual timeline is 10 business days.
